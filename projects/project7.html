Roblox / Lead QA / Desktop

Overview

A fast-paced 3v3 football experience on Roblox, created to capture the excitement of the World Cup. Players compete in quick team-based matches, showcasing teamwork, skill, and strategy in an accessible and engaging football environment.

Core Features

  • 3v3 Team-Based Matches – Fast-paced football gameplay that emphasizes teamwork, coordination, and quick decision-making.
  • Matchmaking System – Matches players against opponents of similar skill levels to create a fair and balanced competitive experience.
  • Leaderboards – Compete with other players and track performance through ranking systems.
  • Player Stats Progression – Character attributes can be improved over time, allowing players to customize and enhance their performance.
  • Shop System – A dedicated store where players can obtain cosmetic items, gacha, and other in-game content.
  • Mission System – Complete various objectives to earn rewards and support player progression.
