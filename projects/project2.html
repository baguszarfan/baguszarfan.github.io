Mobile Nurturing Game

Nurturing Game / Lead QA / Mobile

Overview

A virtual pet simulation game inspired by classic Tamagotchi-style experiences. Players nurture, care for, and build emotional bonds with their pets through daily activities, progression systems, and long-term growth mechanics.

Core Features

  • Fun Meter – to track the pet's mood and overall well-being.
  • Hygiene Meter – to monitor the pet's hygiene level.
  • Hunger Meter – to indicate the pet's hunger status.
  • Sleep Meter – to show the pet's current energy level.
  • EXP Meter – Pet evolution system that allows the pet to grow from a baby to an adult stage.
  • Various Minigames – A variety of minigames that allow players to earn in-game currency.
