Interactive Football Games

Augmented Reality / Lead QA / Desktop

Overview

An interactive soccer game developed to celebrate the excitement of the 2026 FIFA World Cup. Players test their skills in penalty shootout challenges, aiming for precision, timing, and high scores with time limit.

Core Features

  • AR Motion Detection – Utilizes augmented reality technology to track player movements and interactions in real time.
  • Penalty Shootout Gameplay – Players take part in interactive penalty kick challenges that test timing, accuracy, and precision.
  • Local Leaderboard – Tracks and displays top scores, encouraging friendly competition among players.
  • Streak Combo System – Consecutive successful shots build combo streaks, rewarding consistency and improving score potential.
