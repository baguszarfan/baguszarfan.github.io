Game Culture Assesment

Assessment Game / Lead QA / Desktop

Overview

An online culture assessment platform designed to help organizations evaluate candidate compatibility with company values, work culture, and behavioral expectations during the recruitment process.

Core Gameplay Modes

  • Character Selection – Players can choose a character that matches their preferences.
  • Visual Novel – Throughout the visual novel sequences, players are presented with dialogue choices that are evaluated as part of the assessment process.
  • Minigames – The primary assessment data is gathered from players' behavior and decision-making patterns during minigame activities.

Additional Features

  • Content Management System (CMS) – An administrative dashboard that enables recruiters to review assessment results, initiate recruitment campaigns, and many other things.
