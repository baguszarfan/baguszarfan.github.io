Mobile Last War-style game

Last War-style game / Lead QA / Mobile

Overview

An action-packed progression game where players lead a growing squad through obstacle-filled stages, defeat enemies, and strategically choose paths to maximize their troop count.

Core Features

  • Infinite Level – Players can continue progressing without a maximum level cap, allowing for long-term growth and engagement.
  • Boss Fight – Special boss encounters are available at designated levels, providing unique challenges.
  • Leaderboard – Daily leaderboards and tournament rankings allow players to track their performance and compete for top positions.
  • Shop – Players can purchase various items, including boosters, energy, gacha pulls, and character skins.
  • Missions – A set of missions that reset every day, featuring specific objectives and rewards to encourage regular gameplay.
  • Inventory – A dedicated inventory where players can store and manage items obtained from the shop, missions, and other in-game activities.

Additional Features

  • Content Management System (CMS) – An administrative dashboard that enables operators to manage game content, launch tournaments, ban system, and perform various operational tasks.
  • Analytics – Used to monitor player behavior, progression, retention, and in-game events.
