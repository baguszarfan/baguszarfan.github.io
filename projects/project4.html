Motorcycle Driving Simulator

Simulation Game / Lead QA / Desktop

Overview

A safe motorcycle riding simulation consisting of 5 scenarios designed to test riding skills and knowledge of traffic regulations. At the end of the scenario, the rider’s score will be displayed. This game is played using a modified motorcycle that allows players to control the motorcycle in the game.

Core Features

  • Preparation Before Riding – players select their riding equipment before riding.
  • Vehicle Inspection – players check the condition of the vehicle.
  • 3 Riding Simulation Scenarios – a motorcycle riding simulation is used to test the player’s riding skills.
  • 2 Quiz Scenarios – A quiz simulation is used to test the player’s knowledge of real-world riding.
